4. Technical Feasibility

4.1 Manufacturing Process

  1. Corrugation: Kraft paper passes through heated rollers to form flutes.
  2. Pasting: Glue is applied to flutes and liner paper is bonded.
  3. Creasing & Slotting: The board is cut to size and creased for folding.
  4. Printing: 2-color Flexographic printing for branding.
  5. Finishing: Stitching/Gluing and bundling for dispatch.
